# Repwerks training

Repwerks distinguishes lasting personal context, goal-directed training intent, planned workouts, and performed work.

## Language

**User profile**:
Lasting goals, constraints, equipment-independent preferences, and relevant personal context for training.

**Training program**:
A goal-directed strategy for recurring workouts: weekly structure, priorities, intended workload, and points to review or adjust it. It is not a single workout or a record of performed work.
_Avoid_: Training block, training protocol

**Workout plan**:
A prescription for one workout, reusable or one-off, with exercises, sets, targets, and expected duration.
_Avoid_: Training program

**Workout block**:
An ordered section of a workout plan, either a repeated circuit or a standalone exercise. Its identity links performed sets to the planned section.
_Avoid_: Training block

**Session**:
A record of work actually performed against a workout plan, including changes and unplanned work.
_Avoid_: Workout plan
